    The brand is in every frame.

    Every party gets a name.

    Choco Bliss: their parties have names, like the Foam Party and the Animal Print Party, so every recap feels like a night people missed.

    Name your parties once and every reel has a title.

  4. 5K viewson a host’s selfie invite, one of their most-watched posts

    The host sells the trip.

    Choco Bliss: the host on camera, saying “I want you to join me.”

    You’re already the host. The First Class founder film puts DJ Bogey on camera, inviting people to Cancún himself.

    The recap sells next year.

    Choco Bliss: its caption says “if you want to join the party, secure your spot.”

    Your trip film is that recap, and the last-night reel announces the next trip while everyone’s still in their feelings.
